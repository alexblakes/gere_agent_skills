# Finding participants by genotype in Cohort browser in CloudOS

[Give us feedback on this tutorial](https://www.smartsurvey.co.uk/s/HYOBC5/)

## Searching

The [Cohort Browser application](https://lifebit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CD/pages/167969760/Cohort+Browser) in [CloudOS](../cloudos/) allows you to search for participants by phenotype and genotype filters.

In either the Source or OMOP data model, you can search by [Omics data discovery](https://lifebit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CD/pages/361103371/Omics+data+discovery+in+Genomics+England) and filter by genes, genomic loci, allele frequencies and variant consequences.

## Further use

You can [download or export your cohort](https://lifebit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CD/pages/208175315/Cohort+Overview) from the Cohort Overview page, or you can drop your cohort into an [interactive session](https://lifebit.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/CD/pages/813105209/Interactive+sessions).
