# Data for cancer participants

There is a vast amount of data for cancer participants in 100kGP. This document sums up what is available and points you to the detailed descriptive files on what is there.

## Cancer clinical data

Clinical data is contained in [LabKey](../labkey/). Cancer data include details of the disease and tumour, sampling information and cancer treatments. These tables are summarised in the [Cancer-specific clinical data](../cancer_clinical/) page.

Some of the clinical tables contain the results of our bioinformatic analyses and have their own documentation pages describing the details of what these tables contain and how they were generated:

- [Cancer analysis](../cancer_analysis/)
- [Staging data (cancer)](../cancer_staging/)
- [Cancer tiering](../cancer_tiering/)

[Orthogonal standard-of-care (SOC) test data (cancer)](../soc/) is also available.

## Cancer variants

We have aggregated together all somatic variants into our [Somatic Aggregated Variant Calls](../somAgg/) files.
