# Change or reset your password

The [recover site](https://recover.genomicsengland.co.uk/showLogin.cc) allows you to change your password, or recover a forgotten password.

## Change your password

1. Navigate to the [recover site](https://recover.genomicsengland.co.uk/showLogin.cc).
2. Input your username (eg `jdoe`, not your email address) and existing password.
3. Enter the MFA code from your Microsoft Authenticator App, enter the Captcha and click Continue.
4. Choose a new password, then reenter it. Enter the Captcha and click Reset Password.

## Forgotten Password

1. Navigate to the [recover site](https://recover.genomicsengland.co.uk/showLogin.cc).
2. Click Forgot your password?
3. Input your username (eg `jdoe`, not your email address) and the Captcha.

   - If you see an error message, send in the ticket to [Service Desk](https://jiraservicedesk.extge.co.uk/plugins/servlet/desk) to reset your password
4. Answer your security questions and enter the Captcha.
5. Enter the MFA code from your Microsoft Authenticator App, enter the Captcha and click Continue.
6. Choose a new password, then reenter it. Enter the Captcha and click Reset Password.
