# Data for all participants

Many of the data tables and files are applicable to all participants: cancer participants, rare disease probands and rare disease relatives. This document sums up what is available and points you to the detailed descriptive files on what is there.

## Clinical data for all participants

Clinical data is contained in [LabKey](../labkey/). There is data on all participants including sampling data, hospital episode statistics and mental health data. These tables are summarised in the [General clinical data](../general_clinical/) page.

## Results of bioinformatic analyses

There are [aggregated variant calls](../aggv2/) of all germline variants, which include functional annotation and principal component analysis, carried out by GEL.

## Public data

The document [publicly available data](../public_data/) lists the public datasets that we have made available in the RE, which you can use to supplement your analysis.
