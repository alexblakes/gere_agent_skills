# 100kGP disease models

Over 190 rare diseases were included in the 100,000 Genomes Project. People invited to take part were believed to have one of these conditions, may have had a provisional diagnosis, but not a molecular diagnosis. A molecular diagnosis is based on a genetic test result rather than on signs or symptoms.

The list of rare diseases included in the project can be obtained from this [pdf](../April_2018_disease_list_approved_version%2012.04.18_WEB.pdf)
