# Prioritise our roadmap for workflows and scripts

We'd like to keep you up-to-date with our plans for future workflows and scripts and get your feedback on those plans. You can take a look at our Productboard below from outside the RE.

## Working with Productboard

Click on the boxes to get more details on our plans. Use the voting option at the bottom to let us know how important this idea is for your research.

Click on the blue plus to submit an idea for us to consider. Please include as much detail as possible, including how this will be useful to you. Please include your email address so that we can get in touch to clarify any details.
